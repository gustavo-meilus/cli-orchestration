# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be precise and kind; critique ideas, evidence, and changes rather than people. Welcome questions, acknowledge uncertainty, respect privacy, and make space for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, intimidation, publishing private information, sustained disruption, or retaliation against good-faith reports.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

Report concerns privately through the repository owner's verified GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers will protect privacy where practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond proportionately. Replace this interim route with a monitored private contact before launch.

This policy is adapted in substance from [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), while remaining intentionally concise.
